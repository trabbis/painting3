Services

Inside and out, one crew handles it.

Every job includes surface prep, floor and furniture protection, two coats where needed, and a full clean-up.

Interior painting

Walls, ceilings, trim, doors and stairwells. Low-odour, low-VOC paints so you can keep living in your home.

  • Patch holes and cracks
  • Caulk trim gaps
  • Cut-in by hand

Exterior painting

Siding, stucco, brick, trim, soffits and fascia. Weather-rated coatings that hold up to sun, rain and freeze-thaw.

  • Power wash & scrape
  • Prime bare wood
  • Seal joints

Cabinet refinishing

Give the kitchen a new look without new cabinets. Doors are degreased, sanded, primed and sprayed for a smooth, hard finish.

  • Doors sprayed off-site
  • Bonding primer
  • New hardware optional

Decks & fences

Cleaning, brightening and staining for decks, fences and pergolas. Transparent, semi-transparent or solid stains.

  • Wash & brighten
  • Sand rough boards
  • Penetrating stain

Drywall & plaster repair

Dents, nail pops, water stains and old anchor holes, fixed and blended so the repair disappears under the paint.

  • Mud & tape
  • Texture matching
  • Stain-blocking primer

Colour consultation

Not sure what colour to pick? We bring sample boards and test patches, and help you choose shades that suit your light.

  • Free with any job
  • Large test patches
  • Finish advice

How it works

From first call to final walkthrough

  1. 1

    Free on-site estimate

    We visit, measure, look at the surfaces and talk through what you want. You get a written, itemised quote within 48 hours.

  2. 2

    Pick colours & a start date

    We help you choose colours and finishes, then book a start date that suits you. No large deposit up front.

  3. 3

    Protect & prep

    Furniture covered, floors draped, fixtures removed. We fill, sand, caulk and prime. Most of a good paint job is here.

  4. 4

    Paint

    Clean cut lines and even coats. We clean up at the end of every day so you can still use your rooms.

  5. 5

    Walkthrough & warranty

    We walk the job with you and touch up anything you spot. Then you get your 2-year workmanship warranty in writing.

FAQ

Common questions

Are estimates really free?

Yes. We come to your home, measure and give you a written quote with no obligation.

Do I need to move my furniture?

Please clear small items and valuables. We move and cover the large furniture ourselves.

How long does a typical room take?

Most bedrooms take one day including prep and two coats. Larger rooms or big repairs can take two.

What time of year can you paint outside?

Exterior paint needs dry weather and temperatures above about 10 °C (50 °F) day and night, so we book exteriors from late spring to early fall.

Do you supply the paint?

Yes, it's included in your quote. If you already have paint you want to use, we're happy to use it.
